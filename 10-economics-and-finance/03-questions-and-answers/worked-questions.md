# Questions and worked answers

Try an answer aloud before opening the explanation. Use the theory chapter for the full derivation; the answers here focus on the reasoning.

## 1. Is nominal growth the same as real growth?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No. Nominal values reflect current prices; real comparisons adjust for price-level changes under a stated method.

</details>

## 2. Why does +10% then -10% lose money in the arithmetic example?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Returns compound: 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99, giving a 1% decline.

</details>

## 3. Does negative price-sales correlation identify demand elasticity?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Not by itself. Confounding, seasonality and simultaneous supply changes can affect both values.

</details>

## 4. What is look-ahead bias?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Using information unavailable at the simulated decision time, such as a revised value or a future closing price.

</details>

## 5. What does opportunity cost measure?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The value of the best alternative forgone by a decision, not merely the cash expense.

</details>

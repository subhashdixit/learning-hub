# Adding learning material

1. Choose the numbered subject.
2. Put explanations in `01-theory`, runnable notebooks/projects and their data in `02-practicals`, and exercises/solutions in `03-questions-and-answers`.
3. Preserve original handwritten PDFs in that subject’s `archive`. Add a typed, illustrated explanation to theory; do not insert PDF page references into the reading flow.
4. Link new material from the relevant index. Avoid empty topic folders and repeated explanations; link to the canonical chapter instead.
5. Keep notebooks beside the datasets/configuration they depend on. Test new worked examples and check local links before publishing.

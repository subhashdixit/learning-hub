## 1. Why is sample SD different from standard error?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

SD describes spread of observations; standard error describes an estimator’s sampling uncertainty. For an independent sample mean, estimate SE with s/sqrt(n).

</details>

## 2. Does p = 0.08 prove H0?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No. It fails to reject at alpha = 0.05 under the test model. Report the estimate and interval; insufficient evidence is not equivalence.

</details>

## 3. Can zero correlation hide a relationship?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Yes. Symmetric x with y = x² can have zero Pearson correlation and a strong deterministic nonlinear relationship.

</details>

## 4. What does a significant ANOVA tell you?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

At least one group mean differs under the model. It does not identify every differing pair; use planned or adjusted follow-up comparisons.

</details>

## 5. Which is categorical: PIN code or purchase count?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

PIN code is a nominal identifier even when numeric-looking. Purchase count is quantitative and discrete.

</details>

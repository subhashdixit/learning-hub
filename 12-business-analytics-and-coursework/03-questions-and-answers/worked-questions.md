Try an answer aloud before opening the explanation. Use the theory chapter for the full derivation; the answers here focus on the reasoning.

## 1. Why is averaging row margins risky?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Rows with very different revenues should not receive equal weight for an overall margin. Divide total profit by total revenue.

</details>

## 2. Why define grain before a dashboard?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It determines valid counts, joins and denominators. Order lines and orders are not interchangeable.

</details>

## 3. Is a hidden dashboard page secure?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No. Apply and test actual data access controls, such as the appropriate row-level policy.

</details>

## 4. Measure or calculated column?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A measure responds to evaluation/filter context; a calculated column produces stored row-level results in the model.

</details>

## 5. How do you make an insight actionable?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

State the decision, supporting comparison, magnitude, uncertainty and a justified next step. Separate observation from explanation.

</details>

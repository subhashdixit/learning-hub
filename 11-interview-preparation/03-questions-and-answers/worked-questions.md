# Questions and worked answers

[Subject home](../README.md) · [Theory](../01-theory/README.md)

Try an answer aloud before opening the explanation. Use the theory chapter for the full derivation; the answers here focus on the reasoning.

## 1. How do you answer an unfamiliar case?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Clarify the decision, target, data availability, baseline and evaluation. State assumptions and work through a small example.

</details>

## 2. When should you look at the solution?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

After an honest attempt and a clear statement of where you are stuck. Then retry with a changed example.

</details>

## 3. What belongs in an error log?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The reasoning mistake, the correct principle and a specific retry task. Avoid copying a long answer without diagnosing the error.

</details>

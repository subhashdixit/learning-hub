## Turn any notebook into a useful exercise

1. Identify the target, prediction time and available inputs.
2. Verify the split and reproduce a simple baseline.
3. Fit preprocessing inside training folds, then compare an appropriate algorithm.
4. Inspect errors, plots, uncertainty and important subgroups.
5. Explain the result and limits in your own words.

Original notebooks/documents are preserved as study material; they may contain historical library APIs, external dataset paths or incomplete exercises. The new labs are the self-contained starting point. Archive PDFs remain available from the subject home.
